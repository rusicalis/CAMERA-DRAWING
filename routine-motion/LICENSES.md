# DAILY ROUTINE motion asset sources

These files are prepared specifically for the DAILY ROUTINE app.

## Vital Animations Free Pack
The following app assets are derived from the official Vital Animations Free Pack downloaded from https://vitalanimations.com/VitalAnimations.zip and used inside this app under Vital Animations' Free Pack usage terms (apps/websites permitted; raw dataset redistribution/resale is not the purpose of this repository).

- `goblet-squat.mp4` — Free50 `0064`, dumbbell goblet squat
- `reverse-lunge.mp4` — Free50 `0059`, barbell reverse lunges (movement guide)
- `shoulder-press.mp4` — Free50 `0094`, kettlebell overhead press
- `lateral-raise.mp4` — Free50 `0095`, cable cross lateral raise (band movement guide)
- `face-pull.mp4` — Free50 `0100`, rear delt cable fly; used by the app as a band rear-delt fly guide
- `running.mp4` — Free50 `0078`, run on treadmill

Vital Animations Free Pack: https://vitalanimations.com/free-pack
